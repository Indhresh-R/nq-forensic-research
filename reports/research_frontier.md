# Research Frontier

The canonical frontier document is maintained here:

[View the current research frontier](../RESEARCH_FRONTIER.md)

The frontier is intentionally conservative. It records which information classes have been tested, which remain data-blocked or unresolved, and which repeats are explicitly prohibited.

## Current principle

Do not reopen a killed hypothesis through parameter shopping, horizon shopping, or relabeling. A new study should introduce a genuinely different information class, objective, or execution mechanism.
