# Hypothesis: Strategy 38 Opening Auction Information

We test whether early cash session behavior (first 5, 10, and 15 minutes of RTH) reveals structural state information that reliably conditions the directional persistence, trendiness, and expansion probability of the remaining session ($T+1 \to 15:55\text{ ET}$).

This is an information-only test to establish whether the market reveals its intraday regime at the open, before attempting any trade execution or monetization.
