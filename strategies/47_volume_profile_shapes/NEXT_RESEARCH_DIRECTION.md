# Next research direction

Step 6 robustness does not support LVN rejection. Stop this object. Do not add another volume-profile trading rule, and do not retune the LVN definitions against a later return.

## 1. What this research has ruled out

- Textbook P / b / D / B labels as the primary description of these NQ profiles.
- Valley depth as a ranking of which boundary matters.
- Preferential traversal: accepted region → LVN → opposite HVN. At H60 and H120 the traverse share is at or below the geometric first-barrier null (Step 4).
- Preferential rejection: accepted region → LVN → return through the approach HVN as a structural reaction above that same null. Unconditional rejection is below the null at every frozen horizon, on both sides, and in both halves of the sample (Steps 5 and 6).

Touching a prior low-volume band is common (39 of 53 eligible next sessions). Frequency of contact is not evidence of a directional reaction.

## 2. Assumptions that remain unsupported

- An LVN is support or resistance beyond the distance to the two surrounding HVNs.
- A same-minute print of the approach HVN is a later reaction to the LVN.
- A decided-path reject share (Null A / Null C) is evidence of a mechanism. Those tests exclude the paths that never resolve, which is where the shortfall lives.
- A narrow slice (`clean` at H120, difference +0.041 on 11 rows) can stand in for the pooled sample.

- HVN–LVN–HVN path direction (traverse and reject), measured from the touch-bar open with frozen horizons H30, H60, H120, and session end.
- Same-minute versus delayed rejection as a rescue of that path claim.
