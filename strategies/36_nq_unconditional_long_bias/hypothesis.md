# Hypothesis: Strategy 36 Unconditional NQ Long Bias

We hypothesize that continuous E-mini Nasdaq-100 (NQ) futures possess a persistent long-side return advantage during the US cash session (09:30--15:55 ET) that remains positive after realistic transaction costs (1.0 index point) without relying on pattern recognition, technical indicators, or selection filters.

Furthermore, we test whether this long-side advantage represents an **active, exploitable trading edge** that beats passive index holding on a risk-adjusted basis, or if it is merely the passive equity risk premium burdened by excessive turnover costs.
