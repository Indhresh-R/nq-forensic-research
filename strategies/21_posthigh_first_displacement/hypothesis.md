# Hypothesis — First post-HIGH directional displacement

## Market behavior under test

Strategy 12 HIGH is often a **recognition point inside** a move, not the start.
~50–55% of (pre + forward) excursion is typically already behind the decision.
Pre-event prediction (13–20) failed.

Hypothesis: after HIGH, the market **reveals** commitment via the first
measurable directional displacement; that sign may predict the remaining path.

```text
HIGH activates
    -> wait for first completed bar
    -> side = sign(close - open)
    -> does remaining expansion prefer that side?
```

This is **not** “momentum predicts momentum” as a pattern hunt.
It asks whether post-event price discovery is informative about what is left.

## What this is not

- Not pre-HIGH displacement (20)
- Not follow at HIGH without waiting (13)
- Not stops / targets / RR / execution research

## Success / failure

### Success

- Material win-lift HIGH−ALL on IS; same-sign Val+OOS; multi-clock
- Residual path at entry still material (report; not used to pick window)

### Failure

- ~50% / no lift / soft single-clock only
- Do not retune bar length from Val/OOS residuals
- Do not open stop/target research to rescue
