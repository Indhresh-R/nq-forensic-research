# Hypothesis — Multi-session residual economics

## Market behavior under test

Conditional on HIGH at T, is the **remaining** unsigned (or signed) excursion
to session end / horizon H large enough after costs to dominate LOW / baseline?

## Economic / mechanical rationale

Strategy 12 shows discrimination; strategy 06/11 showed NY-open HIGH is often
already-moved. Same risk here — especially LONDON’s large gaps. Residual must
be measured from **next open after T**, not from session open.

## Success / failure criteria

### Success

- Material residual Δpts or cost-cover lift vs LOW/baseline on Val+OOS
- Not explained solely by pre-T excursion

### Failure

- Cost-cover Δ ~0; residual negative; year flips
