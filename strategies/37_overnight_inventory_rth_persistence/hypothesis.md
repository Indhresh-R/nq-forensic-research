# Hypothesis: Strategy 37 Overnight Inventory → RTH Directional Persistence

We test whether pre-market overnight inventory states (Globex drift, range compression/expansion, inventory location pinning, and overnight extension relative to prior RTH) contain statistically robust information that alters the subsequent RTH directional persistence distribution.

This is strictly an **information test**: we evaluate whether the market's overnight structural state reliably separates high-persistence directional trend days from low-persistence two-sided chop days, without executing active trading rules.
