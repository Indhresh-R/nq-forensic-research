# Hypothesis: Bull Flags and Bear Flags Continuation

Classical technical analysis claims that after a sharp directional thrust (flagpole), an orderly, low-volume consolidation retracing $\le 50\%$ (flag) provides a high-probability continuation setup upon breakout.

This research tests whether:
1. Bull Flags and Bear Flags produce positive net expectancy after transaction costs on continuous NQ and ES futures.
2. The flag consolidation adds true incremental edge over a naive momentum breakout (pole alone).
3. The classical "measured move" target ($1.0 \times \text{flagpole}$) outperforms fixed risk-reward exits (1.0R, 1.5R, 2.0R).
4. Any observed edge remains stable across In-Sample (2010–2021), Validation (2022–2024), and Out-of-Sample (2025–2026).
