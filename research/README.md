# Supporting Research

This folder contains supporting research material that does not belong to a single numbered strategy dossier.

Use it for source-derived notes, research translations, and other material that informs later preregistration.

## Boundary

- Framework methodology belongs in [RESEARCH_FRAMEWORKS](../RESEARCH_FRAMEWORKS/).
- Formal test methodology belongs in [research_framework](../research_framework/).
- Executable study dossiers belong in [strategies](../strategies/).
- Program-level conclusions belong in [reports](../reports/).

Supporting material is not itself evidence of a trading edge.
