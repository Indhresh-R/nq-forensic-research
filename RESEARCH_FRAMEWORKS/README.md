# Framework Research

This folder contains **framework-discovery material**, not executable strategy results.

The framework research asks whether an established trading methodology can be translated into an explicit:

```text
market state → context → event → reaction → mechanism → falsification
```

without disguising previously tested indicator/level ideas under a new name.

## Current material

- [Framework Discovery](FRAMEWORK_DISCOVERY.md) — structured extraction and feasibility audit of AMT/Market Profile, Wyckoff, VSA, Footprint, and DOM/order-flow frameworks.

Framework research is upstream of strategy research. A framework appearing here does **not** mean it has been validated or found profitable.

See [the research framework](../research_framework/README.md) for the repository's testing methodology and [the research overview](../reports/research_overview.md) for the overall program.
